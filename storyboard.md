# Online Jewellery System - Storyboard

## Problem

Customers may find it difficult to browse, select and purchase jewellery
conveniently. The Online Jewellery System allows customers to view jewellery,
check details and place orders online.

---

## Story 1 - User Registration

**User Action:**  
The customer creates an account by entering their name, email and password.

**System Response:**  
The system creates the customer's account.

**Progress:** Completed

---

## Story 2 - Browse Jewellery

**User Action:**  
The customer views the available jewellery products.

**System Response:**  
The system displays jewellery with images, names, prices and categories.

**Progress:** Completed

---

## Story 3 - Search Jewellery

**User Action:**  
The customer searches for a jewellery item.

**System Response:**  
The system displays matching jewellery products.

**Progress:** In Progress

---

## Story 4 - View Product Details

**User Action:**  
The customer selects a jewellery product.

**System Response:**  
The system displays product details such as price, material, weight and
availability.

**Progress:** In Progress

---

## Story 5 - Add to Cart

**User Action:**  
The customer adds a selected jewellery item to the cart.

**System Response:**  
The system adds the item to the shopping cart and calculates the total price.

**Progress:** To Do

---

## Story 6 - Place Order

**User Action:**  
The customer confirms the items and provides the delivery details.

**System Response:**  
The system creates the order and displays the order confirmation.

**Progress:** To Do

---

## Story 7 - Order History

**User Action:**  
The customer opens the order history.

**System Response:**  
The system displays previous orders and their status.

**Progress:** To Do
